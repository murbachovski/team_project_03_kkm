| 14 | CONVENIENCE | Malbawi Information Center Malbawi Information Center | The Malbawi Information Center is at the entrance to the hiking circuit path of the wall in Baegak Mountain near the Waryong Park. It was closed to the public for 40 years after the North Korean infiltration attempt in 1968, and was reopened in April 2007. In order to enter the area, visitors must provide a proof of their identities as a means to ensure the security of the military facilities.   An ID card such as a resident registration card or a driver's license card must be presented to gain entry. It is open until 4 p.m. between the months of March and October and until 3 p.m. between November and February. |
