| 57 | VIEW | Beombawi Rock of Inwangsan | This is a rock situated near the hill between Nusang-dong and Muak-dong, and it was called Hoam because it was in the shape of a tiger. According to the legend, there was a pair of tigers living on the hillside of Inwangsan Mountain that traveled to Muakjae from time to time, but they never caused any harm to the people. However, whenever there was something causing harm and injury to humans, they would roar at it and drive it away. One day, there was a fire in Inwangsan Mountain that destroyed nearly everything. Nearly starving, the tigers came down to a home on the foothills in search of food, and a hunter from Gangwon-do Province shot and killed the tigress. The tiger, devastated at the loss of his mate, cried and roamed until he hit his head on the Beombawi Rock and died. A piece of the rock broke off and the rock took the shape of the dead tiger. Sunshine that gets reflected from this rock is blinding like the gaze of a tiger, and the hunter who killed the tigress became blinded by the reflected light. |
