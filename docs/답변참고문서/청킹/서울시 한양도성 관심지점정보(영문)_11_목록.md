| 60 | PHOTO | Chimabawi Observatory of Inwangsan | This is a large and flat rock found west of the Sajik Park in Sajik-dong, Jongno-gu. In the early 16th century during the Joseon Dynasty, Jungjongbanjeong (insurrection of King Jungjong) resulted in the accession of Jungjong to the throne. At the time the Queen of Jungjong was the daughter of Sin Su-geun, who was killed during the insurrection, and the subordinates who supported King Jungjong demanded that the Queen be dethroned as she was the daughter of a criminal. King Jungjong had no choice but to dethrone the Queen and make her return to her home. He could not forget the joyful memories of their time spent together over the course of 10 years, so he often went to Gyeonghoeru to observe her home on the foothills of Inwangsan Mountain. Upon learning of this, she hung her dress on one of the highest rocks in Inwangsan Mountain that could be observed from Gyeonghoeru. This story was passed on to the people, and later they began calling the rock where the dethroned queen hung her dress, Chimabawi Rock. |
