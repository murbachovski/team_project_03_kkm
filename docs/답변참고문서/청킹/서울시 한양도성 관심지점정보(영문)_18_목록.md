| 6 | VIEW | Baegakmaru | Baegakmaru, which is the peak of Baegaksan Mountain, is the highest point of the wall; a stone post inscribed with "Baegak, altitude of 342m" stands there.   During the first construction project, the whole length of the wall was divided into 97 sections, which were named in the order of the characters in the Thousand Character Classic. The first section was called Cheon (天; sky) and the last one Jo (弔; condolences). Baegakgmaru is the first ‘Cheon (天; sky)’ section of the wall. |
| 53 | PHOTO | Inwangsan Mountain's Best View Observatory | This is a place offering scenic views located on the hiking circuit path of the wall near the Sajik Neighborhood Park in Inwangsan Mountain. Here, you can see the lines and contours of Seoul City Wall along Inwangsan and Baegak mountains at a glance. |
| 22 | STONE | Dongboksi | Location: outside the wall Number: 53  Dongboksi  Explanation: The section of the city wall at which the reconstruction work was commenced by the military government of Dongbok, Jeolla-do Province (present-day Dongbok-myeon, Hwasun-gun, Jeollanam-do), in 1422 (4th year of King Sejong's reign) |
| 12 | VIEW | Gokseong (Gokjang) | Gokseongs are rounded, projecting sections of the wall which exist in Inwangsan and Baegak. Only Baegak Gokseong is open to the public, and it is one of the best places to admire the mountainous topography of Seoul. |
