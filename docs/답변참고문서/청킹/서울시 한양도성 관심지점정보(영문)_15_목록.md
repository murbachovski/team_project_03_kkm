| 26 | INFORMATION | Heunginjimun Gate Heunginjimun | Heunginjimun is “East Gate” (Dongdaemun) of the wall. The current Heunginjimun was rebuilt in 1869 (6th year of the reign of Emperor Gojong). It has been designated as National Treasure No. 1 as it demonstrates the characteristics of late Joseon architecture.   Due to topography of Seoul, characterized by the western part of the city being built on higher ground and the eastern part is on low ground, Heunginjimun was the most vulnerable to attack from the military perspective. That is why the subsidiary wall were built outside Dongdaemun. In 1907, the walls on the right and left sides of the gate were demolished. |
| 38 | VIEW | Seonggwakmaru | Seonggwakmaru is a small pavilion built on a low-altitude hill at the end of Seoul City Wall, and it offers a scenic view of the southeast part of the city center.   Against the stunning backdrop of Namsan Town above the Yaksudong Hill, you can take in a full view of the Hannam-dong area and Banyan Tree Club & Spa Seoul? as well as the peak of Namsan Mountain. |
