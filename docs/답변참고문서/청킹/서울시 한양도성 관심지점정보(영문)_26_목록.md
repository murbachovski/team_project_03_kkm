| 20 | CONVENIENCE | Naksan Park | As the hiking circuit path is mostly found within the city wall, only the low wall, roughly about the shoulder height of a man, is visible. The hiking circuit path of the wall on the Naksan Trail is arranged outside the wall so that visitors can observe the magnificent, solid structure of the wall at a glance.  This is a great place to enjoy the view of Seoul City Wall and the surrounding scenery, while walking along the trail with a gentle slope. Jangsu Village, situated beneath the Naksan Park to the east, provides a glimpse into the past apperance of Seoul, while the cityscape can be viewed to the west along with Inwangsan and Baegaksan mountains.  Visitors can take a stroll here even at nighttime thanks to the installation of lighting fixtures. At the peak of Naksan Mountain, found at the end of this trail, a panoramic view of Seoul takes one's breath away. |
