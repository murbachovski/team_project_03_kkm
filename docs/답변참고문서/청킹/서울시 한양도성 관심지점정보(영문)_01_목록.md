| 28 | INFORMATION | Battlement - Igansumun Floodgate | Battlements are protruding facilities of a fortress built at constant intervals to defend the area against the enemy forces.   In Korean, these battlements are called ""Chiseong,"" where ""Chi"" means a ""pheasant,"" which is exceptional at hiding itself from potential predators. It is speculated that there were 5 battlements in the Heunginjimun and Gwanghuimun areas. Traces of battlements were found during the excavation work performed in the site of Dongdaemun Stadium and they underwent restoration before being displayed at the Dongdaemun History & Culture Park. |
| 59 | VIEW | Gichabawi Rock of Inwangsan | While walking along Seoul City Wall from Cheongun Park toward the Inwangsan Mountain Trail, you will come across a crossroad that leads to the peak of Inwangsan Mountain and Gichabawi Rock. Gichabawi Rock was named so because when it was viewed from far away, it appeared as though there are a couple of trains here. Of particular note, Inwangsan Gokseong presents picturesque scenery along the ridge of the Bawisan of Inwangsan that exhibits rugged terrains. Gichabawi Rock is where you can also observe the excellent fortification techniques of the Joseon Dynasty that enabled the construction of a city wall upon rugged terrains. |
