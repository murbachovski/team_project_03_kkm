| 17 | INFORMATION | Hyehwamun Gate | It is Dongbukmun (Northeast Gate), one of the four auxiliary gates on the wall. It was originally called Honghwamun at the time of its construction in 1396, but was renamed as Hyehwamun in 1511. The gate tower was demolished in 1928, followed by Hongye (arched door) in 1938. However, Hyehwamun was reconstructed in its current location, a little further north from its original site, in 1994.  Commonly called Dongsomun Gate, Hyehwamun Gate is situated between Dongdaemun and Sukjeongmun gates. Because Sukjeongmun, located on the north, was always closed, Hyehwamun served as the gateway between Hanyang and the northern regions. |
