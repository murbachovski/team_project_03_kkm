| 24 | INFORMATION | Dongdaemun Seonggwak Park | Gakjaseongseok Stone Block is a stone with an inscription of a text regarding the fortification work. Majority of these stones are found next to the Dongdaemun Seonggwak Park because those discovered while repairing Seoul City Wall were collected in this area.   Stone blocks with character envgravings made during the reign of King Taejo and King Sejong each contains the name of the section and the name of the county and prefecture in charge of the fortification of each section. On the other hand, those made after the mid Joseon Dynasty period contain the name of the supervisor and the principal engineer and the date of the fortification. |
| 21 | VIEW | Naksanjeong Pavilion | There is a small pavilion called Naksanjeong situated on the south side of Naksan Park. A picturesque scenery is created by the eaves and pillars of the pavilion and Inwangsan Gokseong. |
