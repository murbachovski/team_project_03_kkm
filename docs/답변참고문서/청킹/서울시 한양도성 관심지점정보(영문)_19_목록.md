| 52 | PHOTO | Woram Neighborhood Park | Seoul City Wall was restored by Seoul Metropolitan Government along the Woram Neighborhood Park. During the establishment of the park, a part of the city wall that had been buried underneath the shaft base of Seoul Welfare Foundation (former building of Korea Meterological Administration) became exposed, and it was later restored with the existing fortification rocks. The restoration work was performed based on speculations made according to the form of the fortification stones that remained in Seoul City Wall. The traces of Seoul City Wall are also found behind the parking lot of row houses near Hong Nan-pa's former home in Hongpa-dong. |
| 18 | VIEW | Backstreet of Catholic University of Korea | This is an area where visitors can observe the shapes of the different stones used in different construction periods as the part of the city wall it contains has been well-preserved.  While walking along the hiking circuit path of the wall, one can witness the magnificence of Seoul City Wall and explore the different construction features unique to the Sejong, Sukjong and Sunjong periods. |
