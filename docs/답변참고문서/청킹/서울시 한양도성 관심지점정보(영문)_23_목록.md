| 32 | INFORMATION | Backstreet of Jangchung Gymnasium | The earth fortifications built during King Taejo's reign were rebuilt using stones.  During King Sejong's reign, the stones were processed into the shape of corn kernel were used for the fortifications, with relatively larger stones comprising the bottom structure and smaller stones comprising the upper structure. The fortification technique unique to the Sejong period can be observed in the backstreet of Jangchung Gymnasium. Also, stone blocks with character engravings can be seen here, and one will see the Sino-Korean characters, ""Saeng (生),"" and ""Gon (崑),"" which are the 42nd and 47th characters of Thousand Character Classic, respectively.   Gyeongsang-do Province was in charge of the fortification of this particular section in the Joseon Dynasty. |
