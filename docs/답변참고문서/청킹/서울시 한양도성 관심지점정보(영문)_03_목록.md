| 39 | INFORMATION | Wooden Stairway | When you walk along the Namsan Beltway extending from the Main Hall Hae to Namsan (Mongmyeoksan) Mountain, you will see a site marking the area which once contained the fortress. Follow that mark and you will see the Wooden Stairway amid the dense forest and shrubs.   The original appearance of Seoul City Wall, built during the reign of King Taejo remains intact. The section of the wall next to the wooden stairway, which runs along the eastern ridge of Namsan, still looks like it did 600 years ago. |
