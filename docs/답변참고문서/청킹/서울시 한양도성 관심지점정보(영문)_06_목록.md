| 47 | INFORMATION | Seoul City Wall Historic Site Exhibition Hal | The Seoul City Wall Historic Site Exhibition Hall displays the ruins of the fortress wall revealed through excavation surveys from 2013 to 2014.  This site is part of the Namsan section of Hanyangdoseong Fortress, and is a section of the fortress wall that has been thought to have been lost.  Namsan Mountain, where the exhibition hall is located, is a place that compresses the long history of Hanyangdoseong.  In the exhibition hall area, which covers the ruins of Hanyangdoseong (1396), the site of the Joseon Shrine distribution (1925), and the Namsan fountain (1969), you can see the history of the construction of the Joseon Dynasty, the hardships of Japanese colonial era, urbanization after liberation, and the recent excavation and maintenance process. |
