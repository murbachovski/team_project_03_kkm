| 11 | VIEW | Ammun~Cheongundae Fortress Area | This is the one and only place on Baegak Mountain Trail where you can observe the external part of Baegak Gokseong wall in detail. It is well-preserved as it remains closed to public access as it is a military zone, and the rectangular and flat shape of the fortification stones demonstrate the Seoul City Wall construction style that’s characteristic of the late Joseon Dynasty period. |
| 45 | PHOTO | Jamdubong Photo Island | The western summit of Namsan called Jamdubong (“Silkworm Head Peak”) because of its shape. The observatory platform on Jamdubong commands a panoramic view of the city, which looks like a dense forest of buildings surrounded by the four inner mountains, and from this, you can estimate the size of Hanyang, the capital of Joseon.  This is the perfect site to take a look at the part of the Seoul City Wall, built along the ridges of Baegak, Inwangsan and Naksan mountains. |
