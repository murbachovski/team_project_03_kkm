| 50 | INFORMATION | Site of Souimun Gate | Souimun, one of the four small gates, is located southwest of Seoul City Wall. It was originally built in 1396 (5th year of King Taejo's reign) as Sodeokmun, but was renamed Souimun in 1744 when the gate tower was rebuilt. The gate was demolished in 1914, and only the stone marker with the name “Souimun” remains. In the past, funeral biers passed beyond the wall via this gate and Gwanghuimun. During Joseon, public executions were carried out in the large yard located outside Souimun. The gate was also called “the gate of martyrs” by foreigners, as many Catholics were martyred outside this gate. The Martyr Memorial Tower in Seosomun History Park is a monument to them. |
