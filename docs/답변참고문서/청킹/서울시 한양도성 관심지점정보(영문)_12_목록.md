| 46 | VIEW | Baekbeom Square | The fountain area next to the Namsan Science Center is the location where the Japanese damaged Seoul City Wall to build Joseon Shrine in 1926.  Seoul Metropolitan Government excavated this area as part of the Seoul City Wall Preservation and Management Project in 2013, and the base of the city wall buried underground was shown to be in a very good condition. This discovery will lead to the compilation of data that provide insight into the fortification techniques used in the Joseon Dynasty and the damaged caused by the Japanese.  Seoul City Wall around the Baekbeom Square has been preserved and restored according to the topographic features of the Sungnyemun Gate area. The sections where restoration of the original structures is impossible due to substantial damaged were marked. |
| 55 | INFORMATION | Entrance of Guksadang Shrine | You'll see a hiking trail hidden behind an iron gate when you walk along the hiking circuit path of the wall from the south side of Inwangsan Mountain. It's the south entrance to the hiking circuit path in the Inwangsan section near Guksadang Shrine. While climbing up this road, you'll come across a wide range of fascinating rock forms including Beombawi, Satgatbawi, Chimabawi, and Gichabawi rocks. |
